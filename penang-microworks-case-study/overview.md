## The Story: Where did Quarter 3 profit go? A budget vs. actual analysis in SQL

Penang Microworks closed Q3 2026 (July–September) with operating profit about 21% below budget. Management wants to know why, and what it means for the full year.

This case study is the monthly variance pack an FP&A analyst would prepare: data quality checks, an FS-format P&L in SQL, a price-volume-mix bridge, a full-year forecast and a short CFO memo explaining the drivers.

## The Company

**Penang Microworks Sdn Bhd** is a fictional Penang manufacturer of electronic modules for industrial and automotive customers.

- **Products:** 6 products in 3 families
  - Sensor modules: high margin (45% budgeted gross margin)
  - Power modules: low margin (25%), a mature and competitive market
  - Connectivity boards: middle (35%)
- **Cost centers:** Engineering, Operations, Sales & Marketing, General & Administrative
- **Size:** About RM98m budgeted revenue for FY2026, 15% operating margin
- **Currency:** Malaysian ringgit (MYR)
- **Period:** FY2026 budget for January–December; actuals for January–September
- **Seasonality:** A dip in February and May respectively (Chinese New Year and Hari Raya shutdowns) and a peak in October–November (pre-holiday builds).

All data is generated by Claude so the company and its numbers are fictional.

## Data quality: issues found in the September GL extract

You've just received the September 2026 GL extract from the ERP. Before building the variance pack, you run your usual checks and hit seven issues. For each one: find the rows, decide the treatment and note it for the README. Every bad row is either quarantined with a reason or kept and labelled, never silently dropped.

## 🔍 Data quality

The raw GL extract was checked before any analysis. Every bad row was either
quarantined with a reason or kept and corrected, never silently dropped.

| Check | Issue found | Rows | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DQ1 Missing department | Aug travel line (RM26,679) with no cost center | 1 | Reclassed to Sales & Marketing, confirmed with the S&M manager |
| DQ2 Future date | Oct 2026 professional fees line in a Sep extract | 1 | Quarantined |
| DQ3 Duplicate | Sep CON-200 revenue batch loaded twice (RM290,737) | 1 | Extra copy quarantined |
| DQ4 Missing mandatory field | Lines with no account or no amount | 2 | Quarantined |
| DQ5 Unmapped account | Account 99 not in the chart of accounts (RM18,500) | 1 | Mapped to new account 6700 Software subscriptions |
| DQ6 Month-over-month spike | June marketing ~3x normal | — | Investigated: genuine trade show, no change |
| DQ7 Revenue reconciliation | GL revenue RM290,737 above sales subledger in Sep | — | Explained by DQ3; ties after quarantine |

**Result:** 635 rows loaded, 4 quarantined, 631 used for analysis.
Total opex is unchanged by the adjustments.

In practice, the DQ1 reclass and the DQ5 account mapping would be posted by accounting in the ERP; they're applied in `sql/adjustments.sql` to mirror that outcome.
